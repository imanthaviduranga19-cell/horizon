About Us

A Legacy of Excellence Since 1980

Horizon Academy has been at the forefront of quality education in Sri Lanka for over four decades — shaping bright minds and building strong character.

Our holistic approach blends rigorous academics with a rich co-curricular life, preparing students not just for examinations but for life itself. We believe every child has unique potential waiting to be unlocked.
